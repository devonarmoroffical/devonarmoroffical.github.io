Meet Devon Armor,

Thanks so much for visiting, or stumbling upon, my portfolio site!

My name is Devon Armor. I'm a journalism and economics student at the University of Maryland with a passion for clear, impactful communication. I am interested in various types of reporting and especially business or data journalism work.

My experience in writing, editing, research, and storytelling has strengthened my ability to translate complex ideas into engaging, accessible content for social media platforms and online webpages. Alongside my editorial interests, I've developed a strong interest in proposal and technical writing. I enjoy using my aforementioned skills to collaborate with these technical teams to craft proposals that help organizations pursue meaningful projects.
